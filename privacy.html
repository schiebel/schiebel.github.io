Privacy policy for nix-tools

nix-tools is a private application with a single user: its owner, who is also its developer. It is used with the open-source program rclone to copy files between the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive.

What the application accesses

With the owner's permission, it can read and write files in the owner's own Google Drive. It uses that access only to copy or mount those files on the owner's own computer.

What happens to data

  • No data is collected by the developer or by any third party.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone else.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking is used.
  • Data is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page, and can delete the credentials in the Google Cloud project.
